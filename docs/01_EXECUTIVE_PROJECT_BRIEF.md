# ShiVi: Executive Project Brief

## Product Positioning
**ShiVi** is the execution layer for distributed teams operating when connectivity and information cannot be trusted.

## Core Architectural Invariant
ShiVi is not an isolated emergency mobile app. It is an **interoperable operational execution network** that ingests alerts from official bodies (NDMA SACHET / CAP, NDEM GIS, IMD), transforms them into localized context, coordinates tasks across disconnected teams with transactional durability, resolves life-safety contradictions through human governance, and exports verified outcomes.

```text
Official Warnings (CAP/SACHET) → Ingestion Gateway → Operations Core → Causal Sync & Conflict Engine → Verified Outcomes & Audit
```

## Strategic Beachhead
Disaster Response & Humanitarian Operations (Cyclone, Flood, Landslide, Earthquake).

## Platform Expansion Horizon
- Public Health Epidemic Field Tracking
- Municipal Water & Road Utilities
- Rural Agriculture Extension
- Infrastructure Defect Inspection
- Distributed Logistics & Freight Operations
